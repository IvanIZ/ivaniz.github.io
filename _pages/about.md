I'm a Robotics PhD student at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. [Sriram Vishwanath](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SOB-2hQAAAAJ&hl=en) and working in close collaboration with Prof. [Glen Chou](https://glenchou.github.io/). My research sits where robot learning meets planning. My ongoing work focuses on latent world models that plan from vision and touch.

I received the MASc degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Waterloo, advised by Prof. [Stephen L. Smith](https://ece.uwaterloo.ca/~sl2smith/index.html). I graduated with a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where I collaborated with Prof. [Kris Hauser](https://kkhauser.web.illinois.edu/) and Prof. [Katherine Driggs-Campbell](https://krdc.web.illinois.edu/).
